AUDIO, WITHOUT THE GUESSWORK

Original files.
Honest quality claims.

Saved Downtify tracks use the original-file endpoint. Unsaved discoveries use Downtify’s full-track cache; server 3.13 prefers the best available M4A, with fallbacks.

That is not a blanket promise of lossless, hi-res or the highest-bitrate codec. EQ, ReplayGain and output settings can also affect the signal.

START WITH YOUR COLLECTION

A few steps to your next listen.

  1. Install the app

    Get the Windows x64 installer from GitHub, or follow the Android guide. The two platforms have separate release versions and feature sets.

  2. Choose your playback engine

    On Windows, install MPV separately and select mpv.exe in Settings. Android bundles its native playback engine.

  3. Connect your sources

    Add your server addresses in Settings, including reverse-proxy base paths. Audiobookshelf uses an API key/access token. Add local audio through the native folder picker.

Your data, your choice.

Credentials use OS-backed encryption. Settings, history and checkpoints stay on your device. Optional lyrics, metadata, scrobbling and Discord integrations contact their configured services. No app analytics service or website tracking is configured.

Ordinary updates retain your sources, settings and cache. No library cleanup or subscription is enabled just by browsing.

Clear limits.

Windows x64 is the desktop target; installers are unsigned. Jellyfin is Direct Play without a server-transcoding fallback. Full Feishin and Audiobookshelf web parity is incomplete. Hardware bit-perfect output, HDR and Dolby Vision are not guaranteed.
